1. What this app does

Media Roulette is a casual party game. It randomly selects photos and videos from the devices of players in a room and shows them to everyone so they can guess who took them.

2. Your responsibilities

You are fully responsible for the media stored on your device and for what you choose to show in the game. Do not use content that is illegal, offensive, harmful, or that you do not have the right to share.

3. No storage of your media

Only the photos and videos played in a game are shared, after you have seen the selection. They are resized and re-encoded on your phone first, which removes location and camera metadata, and encrypted end to end. The game server keeps them only while your game runs and they are deleted when the game ends, when you leave, after 15 minutes of inactivity, or after 3 hours at most.

4. Online features

Multiplayer games use an online connection to sync players, scores and game state. Do not attempt to cheat, disrupt games, or attack the service or other players.
