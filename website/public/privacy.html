Media Roulette

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Media access

The app reads your photo and video library only to pick random media for the game, and only after you allow it. You see the selection first and can swap or remove anything. Only the items actually played in the game ever leave your phone: they are resized and re-encoded on your device first, which removes location and camera metadata. In Time Machine games, the year each photo was taken is also shared, and only revealed after the round.

2. What is stored

The game server keeps only what a game needs while it runs: the room PIN, usernames, answers and scores. There are no accounts, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your data.

3. Multiplayer data

During a game, your username, answers, captions and scores are shared with the other players in the same room, along with the media played. Media is stored temporarily on the game server and deleted when the game ends, when you leave, after 15 minutes of inactivity, and in any case after 3 hours. Phones delete downloaded media at the end of the game.

4. Permissions and control

You can revoke photo and video permissions at any time in your device settings. In the app settings you can also exclude specific albums so they are never used in the game.

5. Security

Media is end-to-end encrypted: it is encrypted on your phone with a key shared only between the players' phones, so the game server stores and relays files it cannot read. Connections to the server are encrypted too.

This policy is a simplified explanation of how the app handles data and may not cover all legal requirements in your country. For full legal compliance, please have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer.
