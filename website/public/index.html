Media Roulette

Whose photo is it?

A free, open-source party game. Everyone's phone picks random photos from their camera roll, and you race to guess who took each one.

App Store: coming soonGoogle Play: coming soon

📸 Your photos, your call

See the selection first, swap anything you'd rather not share, and hold your own photo during a round to hide it.

🔒 End-to-end encrypted

Only the photos actually played leave your phone, encrypted with a key only the players' phones know. Deleted when the game ends.

🎉 Modes and extras

Photos, videos, Blind Mode, Time Machine, Slow Reveal, plus Caption This and Story Time for parties.

🆓 Free, no account

No ads, no account, no tracking. MIT-licensed and self-hostable.

How to play

One person creates a game and shares the PIN, the QR code or an invite link. Everyone joins, checks their random photos, and the guessing starts: 500 points for the right answer, up to 500 more for speed, with a streak bonus.
